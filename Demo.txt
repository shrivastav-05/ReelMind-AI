Salman Khan is one of the most popular and influential actors in the Indian film industry. He was born on 27 December 1965 and made his acting debut with Biwi Ho To Aisi. Salman Khan gained massive fame after the success of Maine Pyar Kiya and later delivered many blockbuster films such as Hum Aapke Hain Koun, Bajrangi Bhaijaan, Sultan, and Kick. He is well known for his strong screen presence, action roles, and emotional performances. Apart from acting, Salman Khan is also a successful television host and producer. He hosts the popular reality show Bigg Boss, which has increased his popularity among television audiences. Salman Khan is also known for his charitable work through his foundation Being Human, which supports education and healthcare. His dedication and star power have made him a true icon of Bollywood.
